Koi Companion Support

Need help with Koi Companion?

Email: koicompanion@gmail.com

Test access

Testing is currently free and invitation-only. Android testers must privately provide the Google account email used on their Android device. iPhone and iPad testers must privately provide the Apple Account email used on their device.

After we add your email to the approved tester list, we will privately send the correct Google Play or TestFlight link. Never post your account email in a public comment. You can request test access from the home page.

After full launch, a subscription fee will be required to help cover ongoing operating and development costs. Subscription options and prices are shown in the app's Subscription section.

What to include when asking for help

  • Your account email address.
  • Your device type, such as iPhone or Android.
  • What you were trying to do.
  • What went wrong.
  • Screenshots if helpful.
  • Whether the issue relates to records, photos, subscriptions, trial access or Koi Companion AI.

Privacy and account deletion

For privacy questions, data requests or account deletion requests, email koicompanion@gmail.com with the subject line Account deletion request or Privacy request.

Fish health note

Koi Companion is not a veterinary service. For urgent, serious or worsening koi health concerns, contact an aquatic vet or experienced koi health professional.
