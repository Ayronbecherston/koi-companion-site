For your club or shop

Encourage consistent dates, units and observations so discussions start with clearer context. A keeper with report access can choose a read-only pond snapshot to share and revoke its link later.

You do not receive automatic access to anyone's account or records. This page introduces the keeper app; it does not promise dealer stock-management tools, a commercial partnership or a professional endorsement.

A simple introduction

  1. Invite the keeper to choose one real task they want to complete.
  2. Let them save it, leave the screen and find it again.
  3. Ask what was saved and what they would do next. Avoid entering invented test values.

Ordinary records do not spend AI credits. New keepers receive 10 welcome AI credits once; routine questions use 1 credit and deep analysis uses 5. Optional Intermediate and Premium plans add wider features and monthly AI allowances. Compare the plans; current local prices and renewal terms are shown before purchase.

AI provides general guidance, not veterinary diagnosis or emergency monitoring. Reports organise a keeper's selected information; they do not certify a pond as healthy.
