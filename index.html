Start free and stay in control

Basic includes 10 welcome AI credits

Ordinary record keeping does not spend AI credits. Every genuine new keeper receives 10 welcome Koi Companion AI credits once. They never expire, but they are not repeated by reinstalling, restoring purchases or switching device. A routine question uses 1 credit; a deep photo, microscope or record analysis uses 5. Once welcome credits are spent, AI needs an optional credit pack or paid plan. Basic record keeping remains free within its limits; not every app feature is included.

Photo, microscope and video analysis require an Intermediate or Premium plan, or optional AI credits. AI gives general decision support and does not replace an aquatic vet or experienced koi-health professional.
