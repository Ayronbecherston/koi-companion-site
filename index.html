Koi Companion

Koi Companion helps koi keepers manage pond records, koi records, water tests, health notes, treatments, reminders, reports and practical Koi Companion support.

The app is launching through a soft public beta with Basic access, a 30-day Premium feature trial and paid Intermediate/Premium subscription options.

Koi Companion provides practical record support and guidance. It is not a veterinary diagnosis service.
