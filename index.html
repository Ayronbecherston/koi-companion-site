How test access works

  1. Choose your platform and privately send the matching Google or Apple Account email.
  2. We add that exact email to the platform's approved tester list.
  3. Once access is enabled, we privately send the correct Google Play or TestFlight link.
  4. Open the link while signed in with the approved account, then install Koi Companion.

Testing now and after launch

Testing is currently free. After Koi Companion launches fully, a subscription fee will be required to help cover ongoing operating and development costs. Subscription options and prices are shown in the app's Subscription section.

About Koi Companion

Koi Companion helps koi keepers manage pond records, koi records, water tests, health notes, treatments, reminders and reports.

Koi Companion provides record support and guidance. It is not a veterinary diagnosis service.
